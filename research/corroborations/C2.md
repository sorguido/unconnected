# C2 — Forwarded-call caller identity

Status: `PHYSICAL TEST REQUIRED`.

## OBSERVED
A third-party call-screening service can use the presented caller URI; Android does not generally expose a trustworthy public origin-of-forwarding identifier to a normal third-party filter. Forwarding operator/PBX configurations may present the original caller.

Source: https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/CallScreeningService

## ASSUMED / UNPROVEN
No particular forwarded office number is confirmed as the presented caller ID on an actual target phone.

## MINIMAL DISCRIMINATING EXPERIMENT
Call an office line that forwards to an Android handset from two different source numbers; record ONLY anonymized result, device OS and PBX behavior. Do not publish actual caller numbers.

## DECISION
The app filters presented caller numbers only. Explain this prominently to avoid false expectations.
