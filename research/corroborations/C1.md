# C1 — Android call screening documentation

Status: `DOCUMENTED` (not independently validated on a physical phone).

Official sources:
- https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/CallScreeningService
- https://developer.android.com/reference/android/telecom/CallScreeningService.CallResponse.Builder
- https://developer.android.com/develop/connectivity/telecom/dialer-app/screen-calls

## OBSERVED
A screening-role app can examine eligible caller IDs and disallow a call, request rejection, or silence it. `setSilenceCall(true)` does not block the call. `setDisallowCall(true)` requests filtering; `setSkipNotification(true)` requests suppression of the missed-call notification. Third-party apps cannot assume suppression of every system log entry. Service must respond within five seconds; known contacts need appropriate permissions.

## PROVEN
Official API capability only: no unconnected APK, telephony result or OEM compatibility has yet been proven.

## DECISION
Use official screening APIs and physically verify with conservative fail-open policy.
